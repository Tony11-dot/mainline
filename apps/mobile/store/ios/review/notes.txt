MainLine is a chess opening trainer. No account is needed: every feature works as a guest, including the opening explorer.

Signing in is optional (Settings → Account) and only syncs your data between devices. There are three options:
• Sign in with Apple: the quickest way to test sign-in, with no chess account needed.
• Sign in with Lichess: opens Lichess in Apple's sign-in sheet. Lichess accounts are free (lichess.org → Register), and Lichess may ask you to confirm your email before signing in.
• Sign in with Chess.com: appears once Chess.com approves our app.

About the previous review (2.1a, Sign in with Lichess on iPad): sign-in used an in-app browser that opened as a small popover on iPad and needed an extra "Open in MainLine?" prompt to return to the app. It now uses ASWebAuthenticationSession, which comes back to the app automatically. We tested the new sign-in sheet on iPad.

Try it: pick a starter repertoire on first launch, then Today → "Learn new moves". Or Repertoire → New → Import PGN.
Reminders use local notifications (Settings → Reminders). Daily streaks are kept on the device.
Native features: local notifications, haptics, share-sheet PGN import ("Open in MainLine"), offline training with an on-device Stockfish engine.
