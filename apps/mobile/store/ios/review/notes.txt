MainLine is an opening-preparation app for chess players. No account is needed: every feature below works as a guest.

What sets MainLine apart (each takes under a minute):

1. Opponent prep. Today → Tools → Opponent prep (or the Games tab, bottom section). Keep "Lichess", type the public username thibault, tap Analyse (reading their games takes up to half a minute). MainLine reads that player's recent games and shows how they answer your first moves and the lines they repeat, each with real game counts and dates, checked against your repertoire. "Add" puts a missing move into your repertoire.

2. Your games against your prep. Games tab → Lichess username: thibault → Import games. Each game shows the exact move where it left your repertoire, and moves you forgot become due again in Today.

3. Building with real-game data. Repertoire → open a repertoire → tap one of your moves. "Their move" shows how often each reply is played at your rating and how it scores, next to master games and an on-device Stockfish check. The Coach tab explains why the move is played.

4. Training. Today → Learn new moves (spaced repetition, FSRS).

On first launch, tap Get started and keep the suggested starting repertoires (Italian Game, Caro-Kann), so steps 1–3 have a repertoire to compare against.

MainLine is our only chess app. It was designed and written for this app; it is not built from a template, an app builder or another developer's code.

Sign-in is optional (Settings → Account) and only syncs data between devices: Sign in with Apple, or Sign in with Lichess (ASWebAuthenticationSession).

Native features: on-device Stockfish engine, offline training, local notifications for due reviews (Settings → Reminders), haptics, share-sheet PGN import ("Open in MainLine"), Sign in with Apple.
